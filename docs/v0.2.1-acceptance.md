## Presentation contract

- Understand, Experiment, and Results remain the only routes and retain their
  V0.2 session, saved-result, stale-draft, keyboard, and responsive contracts.
- A shared quiet product identifier, maximum content width, page gutters,
  editorial hierarchy, and stable vertical rhythm are applied throughout.
- The cream paper surface, navy structure, dark-coral primary action and
  known-answer reference, restrained display serif, unified sans body, and
  exact-value monospace form one centralized semantic system.
- Understand keeps the approved headline at a smaller scale, places its action
  in the first viewport, and adds one deterministic owned explanatory SVG.
- Experiment uses controls followed by one seven-row summary rail: side by side
  on desktop and stacked on mobile. Scenario previews are deterministic
  presentation only.
- Results places the known-answer and method-estimate rail immediately below
  its introduction and brings the primary comparison into the first desktop
  viewport without creating a ranking.
- Method identities use A through D plus names and marks across summaries,
  charts, tables, and the self-contained Safety Card.
- Motion is limited to interaction feedback and is removed under reduced
  motion. No gradients, page transitions, chart animation, dark mode, or new
  frontend infrastructure were added.
- The final layout-alignment pass keeps Streamlit wide mode and replaces the
  centered 1240-pixel outer container with one owned, left-anchored shell. The
  shell uses a 1600-pixel cap, responsive 24-32 pixel gutters, and readable
  internal prose measures.
- Understand uses a 42.5/57.5 hero split and a normal-order 2 by 2 desktop step
  grid that becomes one column on mobile. Each step uses a compact rounded white
  card with a soft outline, a restrained tinted shadow, and its number as the
  top label. The 640-pixel-capped columns separate across the wider shell so 02
  and 04 sit at the right edge. A white boundary note aligns below 03 at the left.
  Experiment uses a 62/38 desktop split. Results, including the known-answer
  rail and charts, uses the wider shell.
- At 1440 pixels, the content edge is x=24 and the Understand title begins
  44.7 pixels below the bottom of the navigation link. Equivalent alignment
  assertions pass on the Experiment and Results pages.
